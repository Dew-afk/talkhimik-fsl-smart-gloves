Calibration wizard

Idle. Start walks thumb → pointer → middle → ring → pinky, flat then 90° each.

IMU mount calibration (glove selected above)

  1. Calibrate Gyro: set the glove down or hold it completely still for 2 s.
  2. Capture Pose 1: palm down, back of hand up, fingers forward, hand level. Hold still 2 s.
  3. Capture Pose 2: fingers pointing straight up. Hold still 2 s. The glove then solves and saves R_MOUNT for this hand.

Live check: hold each pose and look at its row. Gravity (az) is estimated from roll and pitch because the telemetry packet carries no raw accel. The motion rows check the last 3 s and the still row checks the last 10 s.

Gesture classifier rules (editable)

Threshold rules per hand, evaluated against the smoothed finger angles. min/max bound specific fingers (t=thumb, p=pointer, m=middle, r=ring, y=pinky). First match wins.
